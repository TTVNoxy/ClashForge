About Clash Royale

Clash Royale is a real-time strategy video game developed and published by Supercell. The game combines elements from collectible card games, tower defense, and multiplayer online battle arena.

Players battle in 1v1 or 2v2 matches, deploying troops, spells, and buildings from their customizable deck of 8 cards to destroy their opponent's towers.

This website will provide you with useful information, card details, and the current game status (simulated).

Featured Cards

Knight

A tough melee fighter. The Barbarian's handsome, cultured cousin. Rumor has it that he was knighted based on the sheer awesomeness of his mustache alone.

Archers

A pair of lightly armored ranged attackers. They'll help you take down ground and air units, but you're on your own with hair styling!

Giant

Slow but durable, the Giant is a powerful tank. He can soak up a lot of damage and is great for leading an offensive push.
