About Clash Royale

Clash Royale is a real-time strategy video game developed and published by Supercell. The game combines elements from collectible card games, tower defense, and multiplayer online battle arena.

Players battle in 1v1 or 2v2 matches, deploying troops, spells, and buildings from their customizable deck of 8 cards to destroy their opponent's towers.

This website, Clash Forge, aims to be your tactical hub, providing useful information, card details, tips, and the current game status (simulated via API).

Tips & Advice

Manage Your Elixir

Don't just spam troops! Pay attention to your Elixir bar. Try to make positive Elixir trades by countering your opponent's cards with cheaper ones. Saving Elixir can allow you to build a powerful push.

Know Your Card Roles

Understand what each card in your deck is for. Some are for defense (e.g., Inferno Tower), some for offense (e.g., Hog Rider), and some are versatile. Knowing their strengths and weaknesses is key.

Counter Push Effectively

After successfully defending an opponent's attack, use your remaining defensive troops to start a counter push. Adding a tank or a high-damage unit in front of them can turn defense into a dangerous offense.
